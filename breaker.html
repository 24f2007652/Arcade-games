HOW TO PLAY

Bounce the ball into every brick. Keep it above your paddle. Clear the wall to advance to the next level.

  • Mouse or ← → moves paddle.
  • Touch and drag on the board.
  • Catch a falling power-up for a wider paddle, a slower ball, or an extra life.
  • P pauses. You start with three lives.
